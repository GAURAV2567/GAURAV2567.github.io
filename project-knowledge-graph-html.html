People Analytics Knowledge Graph

Developed a ground-up clone of the NASA People Analytics Framework during tenure at Entomo (KPISoft) — taken from concept to pre-production. The system maps skills, occupations, training records, and mission data into a unified Neo4j knowledge graph, improving internal search speed by ~40%.

  • Comprehensive data integration across employee, occupation, training, and mission data
  • Advanced skills identification using Doc2Vec and NLP extraction from textual sources
  • Dynamic relationship mapping between employees, skills, and career paths
  • Predictive analytics for skills gap analysis and succession planning
  • Internal talent marketplace — CV vs. job requirement matching
  • Strategic workforce alignment across projects and missions
